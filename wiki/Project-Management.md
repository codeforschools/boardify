## Commenting on Edits

As mentioned, Github serves three main functions: source control, hosting, and project management.  The following relates to that last point, specifically in how we manage edits themselves.

### 1. General Comments on the Overall Topic

Pull Requests can often encompass multiple files, most often a main policy with several related pull requests.  To make these types of comments (which can include general status updates, scheduling, agenda recommendations, etc) click on the "Conversation" tab (which is the default tab when clicking on the Pull Request itself).

Then scroll to the bottom and add a comment.
<img width="600" height="579" alt="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 08 22 19" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/10d687a5-801d-4a92-a6d6-335f7376091c" />


### 2. General Comments on a Specific File

To make a general comment on one specific file, click on the "Files Changed" tab on the right.
<img width="600" height="579" alt="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 08 24 35" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1c56ba44-57f2-4c3d-99c9-7d99538381ff" />

This takes you to a list of the changed files.
<img width="600" height="579" alt="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 08 24 26" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/358d6396-7135-42f5-a910-2c16a6f2d474" />

Scroll down to the file on which you wish to comment, and click the comment bubble icon.
<img width="600" height="579" alt="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 08 26 02" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4cad0688-2a4d-458e-af59-58d3c5e676cf" />

Make your comments and click "Comment" or "Start a Review".

**NOTE:** Both will save your comment and send it to all the relevant participants in the field; the only difference is that clicking "Comment" will send it right away, while "Start a Review" will batch up all comments and send them as a whole review.  The former is great for one-offs; the latter is best if you're doing an overall review and expect to comment on multiple files.

<img width="600" height="579" alt="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 08 28 37" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e4056a7a-cca5-4586-b7f7-9170fbf88e44" />



### 3. Specific Comments on a Specific File

To make specific comments on a specific file, you use the same approach as #2 to find the file you wish to edit.

Then, mouse over the line that you wish to make comments on.
<img width="600" height="579" alt="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 08 34 18" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e17715d5-4f1e-4c25-8b20-7fb029680b51" />


Click the "Plus" icon, and a window will open up, allowing you to make a comment on that specific line.
<img width="600" height="579" alt="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 08 34 20" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/36c67e9a-df32-4775-bd19-ae049da452cf" />

Click either "Comment" or "Review" to save.  See #2 for the differences between them.
<img width="600" height="579" alt="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 08 34 25" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a6b43799-c050-4872-a571-b3bf4fb83c11" />


Don't worry too much about putting comments in the wrong place.  The most important thing is to get them somewhere in Github so that we don't have to search around for comments in email.  The goal is to keep the conversation all in one sharable place.
